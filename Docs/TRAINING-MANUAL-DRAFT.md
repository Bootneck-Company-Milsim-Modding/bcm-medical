# BCM Medical training guide

The current player and instructor manual is [BCM Medical Player Training Guide](Training/BCM%20Medical%20Player%20Training%20Guide.docx).

Updated 30 October 2026 against the player-focused implementation and canonical catalogue: 29 supplies, 279 settings, schema 35. The guide covers treatment workflows, equipment, carry/drag consent, sling retention, deceased recovery, lives, suppression, focus, persistence and instructor drills. Shelved and unfinished features are identified separately.

The [superseded development draft](History/TRAINING-MANUAL-DRAFT-20261030-SUPERSEDED.md) is retained for historical reference only; its status statements, counts and procedures must not be used for current training.
